About

I'm an economist and financial analyst. Most of my work sits where financial reporting meets economic analysis: taking figures from many entities in a group, reconciling them to the underlying statements, and turning them into something a regulator will accept.

At PwC Canada I prepare transfer pricing documentation for multinational groups in manufacturing, technology and distribution, draft submissions to the Canada Revenue Agency, and automate the data cleaning and validation behind the analysis in Excel. Before that I built and maintained the public economic indicators, databases and Power BI dashboards at the Saskatoon Regional Economic Development Authority.

I hold an MA in Economics from the University of Saskatchewan, and I work in English, French, Persian and Azerbaijani.

Experience

    PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) Canada, May 2024 to present
