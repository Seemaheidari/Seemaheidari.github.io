  • Emissions, energy use and growth across Canadian provinces

    Time series econometrics in R, 1997-2019, published as a reproducible Quarto site

    Eleven jurisdictions tested separately for a long-run relationship between greenhouse gas emissions, industrial energy use and real GDP. Ontario is the only province where both cointegration tests agree there is one, and emissions do not predict economic growth anywhere. Rebuilt in R from a 2023 Master's project whose original Stata code was lost.
