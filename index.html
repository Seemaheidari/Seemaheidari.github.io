Economic analysis and financial reporting for multi-entity groups

I'm an economist and financial analyst. I pull financial information together from many entities and report it to a standard regulators accept. At PwC Canada I prepare transfer pricing documentation for multinational groups, reconcile intercompany figures to the underlying financial statements and draft submissions to tax authorities, automating the data cleaning and validation along the way. Before that I maintained the monthly economic indicators, databases and Power BI dashboards at the Saskatoon Regional Economic Development Authority.
